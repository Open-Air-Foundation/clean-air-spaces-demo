How the estimates work

Each place has 1–5 simulated measurements, each pairing an indoor reading with an outdoor reading taken nearby at the same time, as in the AirGradient Go measurement flow.

The ratio between inside and outside tells how much outdoor smoke a place keeps out. The estimate on the map is that ratio applied to the outdoor air at the place right now.

Clean Air Space means at least two measurements, each at most 30% of outside, and at least one taken on a polluted day.

About 40% of Clean Air Spaces are fully sealed: they stay below 5 µg/m³ inside whatever the outdoor level.

About this prototype

Place names and locations are real, from OpenStreetMap. All air quality values are simulated and do not describe these places.
